## About HB Artificial Intelligence

**HB Artificial Intelligence** is the artificial intelligence, cybersecurity and intelligent technology division of **Hephzibah Behulah**.

We develop intelligent software, educational platforms, cybersecurity resources and digital solutions that help individuals, organizations and communities understand and use modern technology effectively.

Our mission is to make artificial intelligence and cybersecurity more accessible, practical, secure and responsible.

## Our Products and Development Areas

### 1. AI Applications

Intelligent applications designed to assist with productivity, learning, research, data processing and everyday problem-solving.

### 2. Cybersecurity Learning Platform

A beginner-friendly educational platform covering cybersecurity fundamentals, computer networks, operating systems, security controls, threats and defensive security practices.

### 3. AI Assistants

Task-focused intelligent assistants designed to support research, information organization, learning and approved automation workflows.

### 4. Security Analysis Tools

Defensive tools for analyzing security information, identifying potential risks and improving awareness of system vulnerabilities.

### 5. Intelligent Automation

Software solutions for automating repetitive tasks, improving workflows and supporting data-driven decision-making.

### 6. AI and Cybersecurity Research

Educational publications, practical experiments, technical documentation and research resources exploring artificial intelligence and digital security.

*These are product and research areas. Availability and release status will be documented individually.*

## Responsible AI and Cybersecurity Standards

1. Use AI responsibly and transparently.
2. Protect user privacy and sensitive information.
3. Do not expose passwords, API keys or access tokens.
4. Develop cybersecurity tools for authorized defensive and educational use.
5. Require appropriate authorization before testing systems.
6. Evaluate AI-generated information for accuracy and reliability.
7. Apply human review to important automated decisions.
8. Respect intellectual property and applicable laws.
9. Maintain appropriate security testing and documentation.
10. Keep independent applications separate from the brand website.
